Ports and cables

Check the ports and display modes of the computer and monitor. The interface and cable must have enough bandwidth for the resolution and refresh rate you need.

HDMI
A digital video and audio connection common on monitors, game consoles, and TVs.
DisplayPort
A digital video and audio connection common on desktop graphics cards and monitors.
USB-C with DisplayPort Alt Mode
A USB-C port that supports this mode can carry video. Compatible devices can also use the same port for USB data and power.
VGA
An analog video interface found on some older devices.
