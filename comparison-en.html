Choosing a monitor

Start by deciding what you use the computer for. Compare monitors and check that the video adapter supports the resolution and refresh rate you need.

UseWhat to compare firstBenefit
Study and office workText clarity, adjustable stand, portsText is easy to read, and you can position the screen comfortably for long sessions
GamingRefresh rate, response time, variable refresh rateMotion looks smoother, with less blur and screen tearing
Photography and designColor gamut, color accuracy, screen uniformityColors are more accurate and brightness is more even across the screen
Movies in a dark roomNative contrast and black levelShadow detail is easier to see in dark scenes

Before you choose

  • Choose a screen size and resolution that suit your desk and viewing distance.
  • Check which display modes your computer and monitor both support.
  • Check how the stand adjusts and which cable is included.
