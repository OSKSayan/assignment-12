Specifications

Resolution describes how much detail an image can show. Refresh rate tells you how often the display updates it. Other specifications also affect the image.

SpecificationMeaningExample
Diagonal sizeDistance between opposite corners of the screen24 or 27 inches
ResolutionNumber of pixels across and down the screen1920 × 1080, 2560 × 1440, 3840 × 2160
Refresh rateNumber of times per second the screen refreshes60 or 144 Hz
Response timeTime a pixel takes to move from one brightness level to another5 ms from gray to gray
BrightnessScreen brightness measured in candelas per square meter300 cd/m²
Contrast ratioRatio of white to black brightness under specified conditions1000:1 native contrast
Color gamutRange of colors the monitor can display99% sRGB coverage

At the same screen size, higher resolution means more pixels per unit area. A higher refresh rate makes motion smoother when the video source supplies enough new frames.
